## 🔥 Top Story

### Welcome to Agents Week

On August 2, 2026, Cloudflare published a kickoff post for "Agents Week" on its blog, written by Rita Kozlov. The post reframes the question "what is an Agent Cloud?", arguing the right move is to ask agents themselves what they need rather than have humans answer on their behalf. It contends today's cloud and web assume a human is watching — attention-grabbing pages, click-through dashboards, interfaces paced to human reading and decision-making — while agents don't get distracted or fatigued and have different needs around speed, structure, and access. An "Agent Cloud," it argues, must do two things at once: build agent-native primitives from the ground up instead of retrofitting human tools, and act as a translation layer between today's human-shaped web and an emerging agent-shaped one. Cloudflare previews five daily themes for the week: execution and storage primitives, an "ADLC" (agentic development lifecycle — SDLC with humans taken out of the loop), secure access controls for employees and agents within organizations, the agentic web, and grounding the ideas in today's reality. It closes by inviting readers to ask their own AI agent what it needs from an agent cloud and share the responses.

> 💡 **Why it matters**: This post has no concrete product announcement of its own — it's framing for the week ahead — but the five previewed themes (execution/storage primitives, org-level access controls for agents) signal where Cloudflare's Workers/agent platform roadmap is headed, so it's worth tracking this week's follow-up posts for the actual specs (agent runtimes, auth/access mechanisms).
